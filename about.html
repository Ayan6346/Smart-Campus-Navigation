About the system

Designed to simplify campus navigation at ITM University

The Smart Campus Navigation platform brings campus locations, facilities, and important services under one user-friendly interface.

Purpose

This application helps students, staff, and visitors find campus buildings, amenities, and sports zones quickly and confidently.

Approach

By combining interactive maps with searchable location data, the system reduces confusion and improves campus accessibility.

Build

Developed using modern HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, the site is responsive and optimized for desktop and mobile browsing.
